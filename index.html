How an order runs

Four steps, about
two weeks, start to stand.

Custom cakes are built in order — each stage needs the one before it, so we keep the runway realistic.

01

Consult

15-minute call or message thread — your date, guest count, and the flavors you're drawn to.

02

Design

A sketch and flavor pairing come back within 48 hours, with one round of revisions included.

03

Bake

Layers are baked, filled, and crumb-coated the week of, then finished the day before pickup.

04

Deliver

Studio pickup or local delivery in a fitted box, timed to the hour you need it standing.

Philosophy

One cake at a time, actually means one cake at a time.

We take a limited number of orders each week so every layer gets baked the day it's meant to, not batched ahead and frozen. Flavors shift with what's ripe; frosting is whipped to order, never piped from a bucket made days earlier.

No fondant, no shelf-stable finishes — just butter, fruit, and a piping bag, built to look like it was made by hand, because it was.
